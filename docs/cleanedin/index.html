Declutter your LinkedIn feed

CleanedIn is a focused Chrome and Edge extension for reducing feed noise.

Filter out promoted posts, suggestions, engagement-only updates, and other low-signal content using compact controls that run locally in your browser.

Add to Chrome Add to Edge

What you can filter

Category Filters

Ads/promoted, suggested, recommended, reposted, followed, and media-specific post types.

Identity Filters

Connection level (following, 1st, 2nd, 3rd+) and profile type (individual, company, group).

Keyword Filters

Hide posts based on include/exclude keyword rules and named-account filters.

Age Filter

Hide posts older than your selected maximum age in days.

Privacy-first design

CleanedIn does not collect your feed data and does not send your browsing content to developer servers. Filter settings are stored in browser extension storage.
